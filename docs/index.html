Local.
Three iOS apps. Your files. No cloud.
LocalGallery
Read-only photo and video viewer for local files. No importing, no library lock-in.
  • Browse folders with cover thumbnails and sorting
  • Collections auto-grouped by tag — people, places, objects
  • Video and Live Photo inline playback
  • EXIF metadata: camera, lens, exposure, GPS
  • Hierarchical keyword tags (XMP/IPTC)
  • No account, no server, no tracking

LocalContacts
File-based contact manager. Each contact is a plain .vcf file in a folder you control.
  • Standard .vcf format — readable by any text editor
  • Optional sync with Apple Contacts for caller ID and QuickType
  • Conflict detection for external edits and deletions
  • Full fields: name, org, phone, email, address, birthday, photo, notes
  • Tag-based filter and search
  • No account, no server, no tracking

LocalMusic
Music player for locally stored audio files. Point it at a folder and it becomes your library.
  • Folder-based library scanned recursively
  • Synced and unsynced lyrics with auto-scroll
  • M3U and PLS playlist support
  • Lock screen controls and Now Playing info
  • MP3, M4A, FLAC, AIFF, Opus, and more
  • No account, no server, no tracking

Works great with Syncthing

All three apps work with any folder accessible to iOS — including folders synced by Syncthing. On iOS, use SyncTrain to run Syncthing on your device. Your files stay on your own hardware, synced directly between devices — no third-party servers involved.
