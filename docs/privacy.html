Privacy Policy

Last updated April 2026

None of these apps collect, transmit, or store any data outside your device. Ever.

LocalGallery, LocalContacts, and LocalMusic make no network connections. There are no analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services of any kind. The apps have no servers to send data to.

Per-app details

LocalGallery

Reads image and video files from a folder you select. Caches thumbnails to disk for performance. Reads EXIF and XMP metadata embedded in your files. No data leaves your device.

LocalContacts

Reads and writes .vcf files in a folder you select. If you grant Contacts permission, it syncs with Apple Contacts on-device — this sync never goes through any server. Contacts permission is optional; the app works fully without it. No data leaves your device.

LocalMusic

Reads audio files from a folder you select. Reads metadata tags (title, artist, album, artwork, lyrics) embedded in your files. Caches the library index to disk for performance. No data leaves your device.
